# 工作压力对员工离职意愿的影响研究：工作满意度的中介作用与组织支持的调节效应

## 中文摘要

在竞争日益激烈的市场环境下，工作压力已成为困扰现代职场人士的普遍问题。过高的工作压力不仅损害员工身心健康，还可能引发离职意愿上升、人才流失加剧等组织层面的负面后果。然而，工作压力如何通过影响员工心理状态转化为离职意愿，以及何种因素能够缓冲压力的消极影响，仍是值得深入探讨的学术议题。

本研究以工作要求-资源模型（JD-R Model）为理论框架，构建"工作压力—工作满意度—离职意愿"的中介模型，并引入组织支持感知作为调节变量，探讨其在压力-满意度关系中的缓冲作用。研究以华东地区服务业企业员工为调查对象，采用问卷调查法收集数据。共发放问卷520份，回收有效问卷438份，有效回收率为84.2%。运用SPSS 26.0与PROCESS宏进行数据分析与假设检验。

研究结果表明：其一，工作压力对离职意愿具有显著正向影响（β=0.46, p<0.001），验证了高工作压力员工更倾向于产生离职念头。其二，工作压力对工作满意度具有显著负向影响（β=-0.52, p<0.001），表明压力会显著降低员工的工作满足感。其三，工作满意度在工作压力与离职意愿之间起部分中介作用，中介效应值为0.19，占总效应的41.3%。其四，组织支持感知显著调节工作压力与工作满意度的关系（β=0.14, p<0.01）：在高组织支持条件下，工作压力对满意度的负面影响被显著削弱。

本研究的理论贡献在于：整合了JD-R模型的核心思想，验证了压力作为"工作要求"通过损耗员工心理资源（满意度下降）导致离职意愿的路径；同时揭示了组织支持作为"工作资源"的缓冲机制。研究结论对企业员工关怀与压力管理实践具有指导意义：企业应在关注任务绩效的同时，重视员工压力水平的监测与干预，并通过增强组织支持来缓冲压力的消极效应，降低人才流失风险。

**关键词**：工作压力；离职意愿；工作满意度；组织支持感知；JD-R模型

## Abstract

In an increasingly competitive market environment, work stress has become a universal problem plaguing modern professionals. Excessive work stress not only damages employees' physical and mental health but may also trigger negative consequences at the organizational level, such as increased turnover intention and accelerated talent loss. However, how work stress transforms into turnover intention through affecting employees' psychological states, and what factors can buffer the negative effects of stress, remain academic issues worthy of in-depth exploration.

Using the Job Demands-Resources Model (JD-R Model) as the theoretical framework, this study constructs a mediation model of "work stress—job satisfaction—turnover intention" and introduces perceived organizational support as a moderating variable to explore its buffering effect on the stress-satisfaction relationship. The study surveyed employees of service industry enterprises in East China and collected data through questionnaire surveys. A total of 520 questionnaires were distributed, and 438 valid questionnaires were recovered, with an effective recovery rate of 84.2%. SPSS 26.0 and PROCESS macro were used for data analysis and hypothesis testing.

The results show that: First, work stress has a significant positive effect on turnover intention (β=0.46, p<0.001), verifying that employees with high work stress are more likely to have turnover thoughts. Second, work stress has a significant negative effect on job satisfaction (β=-0.52, p<0.001), indicating that stress significantly reduces employees' job satisfaction. Third, job satisfaction partially mediates the relationship between work stress and turnover intention, with a mediation effect value of 0.19, accounting for 41.3% of the total effect. Fourth, perceived organizational support significantly moderates the relationship between work stress and job satisfaction (β=0.14, p<0.01): under high organizational support conditions, the negative impact of work stress on satisfaction is significantly weakened.

**Keywords**: Work Stress; Turnover Intention; Job Satisfaction; Perceived Organizational Support; JD-R Model
