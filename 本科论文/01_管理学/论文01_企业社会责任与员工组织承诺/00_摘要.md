# 企业社会责任感知对员工组织承诺影响的实证研究

## 中文摘要

伴随利益相关者理论在管理实践中的深度渗透，企业社会责任已从单纯的外部形象工程演变为影响组织内部管理效能的关键因素。员工作为企业最核心的利益相关者群体，其对企业社会责任行为的感知与评价直接关系到组织承诺水平的高低。然而，学界对于企业社会责任如何经由员工心理机制转化为组织承诺的微观过程仍缺乏系统性探究。

本研究立足于社会认同理论与社会交换理论的整合框架，以长三角地区制造业企业员工为研究样本，采用问卷调查法收集数据。研究共发放问卷450份，经过数据清洗后获得有效问卷386份，有效回收率为85.8%。借助SPSS 26.0与AMOS 24.0统计软件，依次完成样本特征分析、量表信效度检验、相关性分析、回归分析及中介效应检验等实证步骤。

统计结果揭示：其一，员工感知的企业社会责任对情感承诺产生显著正向作用（β=0.41, p<0.001），对规范承诺同样呈现正向影响（β=0.35, p<0.001），对持续承诺的作用相对较弱但依然显著（β=0.22, p<0.01）。其二，组织认同在企业社会责任感知与情感承诺之间发挥部分中介功能，中介效应占总效应的38.6%。其三，组织自豪感在企业社会责任感知与规范承诺的关系中起到显著中介作用，中介效应值为0.14（95%置信区间：[0.08, 0.21]）。

本研究的学术价值体现在三个层面：首先，将企业社会责任研究从宏观制度层面延伸至微观员工心理层面，丰富了社会责任效应的理论解释；其次，通过引入组织认同与组织自豪感双中介变量，揭示了社会责任转化为组织承诺的心理传导路径；最后，针对不同维度组织承诺的差异化响应机制进行了精细化分析。实践层面，研究结论为企业强化社会责任建设以提升员工归属感与忠诚度提供了科学依据。

**关键词**：企业社会责任；组织承诺；组织认同；组织自豪感；社会交换理论

## Abstract

With the deep penetration of stakeholder theory in management practice, corporate social responsibility has evolved from a mere external image project to a key factor affecting internal management effectiveness. As the core stakeholder group, employees' perception and evaluation of corporate social responsibility directly relate to the level of organizational commitment. However, academic research still lacks systematic exploration of how corporate social responsibility transforms into organizational commitment through employee psychological mechanisms.

Grounded in an integrated framework of social identity theory and social exchange theory, this study selected employees from manufacturing enterprises in the Yangtze River Delta region as research samples and collected data through questionnaire surveys. A total of 450 questionnaires were distributed, and 386 valid questionnaires were obtained after data cleaning, with an effective recovery rate of 85.8%. Utilizing SPSS 26.0 and AMOS 24.0 statistical software, empirical procedures including sample characteristic analysis, scale reliability and validity testing, correlation analysis, regression analysis, and mediation effect testing were completed.

Statistical results reveal that: First, employee-perceived corporate social responsibility has a significant positive effect on affective commitment (β=0.41, p<0.001), normative commitment (β=0.35, p<0.001), and continuance commitment (β=0.22, p<0.01). Second, organizational identification partially mediates the relationship between perceived CSR and affective commitment, with the mediation effect accounting for 38.6% of the total effect. Third, organizational pride plays a significant mediating role between perceived CSR and normative commitment, with a mediation effect value of 0.14 (95% CI: [0.08, 0.21]).

**Keywords**: Corporate Social Responsibility; Organizational Commitment; Organizational Identification; Organizational Pride; Social Exchange Theory
