# 变革型领导对员工创新行为的影响机制研究：心理授权的中介作用

## 中文摘要

在知识经济与技术变革加速演进的当代商业环境中，员工创新行为日益成为组织获取持续竞争优势的关键驱动力。作为影响员工态度与行为的重要情境因素，领导风格对员工创新的激发作用已引起学界的广泛关注。变革型领导以其愿景激励、智力激发、个性化关怀等特征，被普遍认为能够有效促进员工的创新意愿与创新产出。然而，关于变革型领导如何通过员工心理状态的中介转化为创新行为，现有研究仍存在探讨空间。

本研究以心理授权理论为核心分析框架，系统考察变革型领导对员工创新行为的影响路径。研究以珠三角地区科技型企业知识员工为调查对象，采用多时点问卷调查设计，在两个时间点分别收集自变量与因变量数据。最终获得有效配对样本298份。借助SPSS 26.0与Mplus 8.3统计软件，完成描述性统计、测量模型检验、结构方程模型分析等实证程序。

实证分析结果揭示：其一，变革型领导对员工创新行为产生显著正向效应（β=0.43, p<0.001），表明高变革型领导能够有效激发下属的创新表现。其二，变革型领导对心理授权的四个维度（工作意义、自我效能、自主性、影响力）均呈现显著正向作用。其三，心理授权在变革型领导与员工创新行为之间发挥显著中介功能，中介效应值为0.21（95% Bootstrap置信区间：[0.14, 0.29]），占总效应的48.8%。进一步的维度层面分析发现，工作意义感和自我效能感的中介效应相对更强。

本研究的理论贡献在于：首先，从心理授权视角揭示了变革型领导影响员工创新行为的内在机理，深化了对领导-创新关系的理论理解；其次，通过维度层面的精细化分析，识别了心理授权各成分在传导领导影响中的差异化作用；最后，为科技型企业情境下的领导行为研究提供了实证积累。研究结论对企业创新管理实践具有指导意义，提示管理者应通过变革型领导行为赋能员工，激发其内在创新动力。

**关键词**：变革型领导；员工创新行为；心理授权；知识员工；中介效应

## Abstract

In the contemporary business environment characterized by accelerating knowledge economy and technological change, employee innovative behavior has increasingly become a key driver for organizations to gain sustained competitive advantages. As an important contextual factor affecting employee attitudes and behaviors, leadership style's role in stimulating employee innovation has attracted extensive academic attention. Transformational leadership, with its characteristics of inspirational motivation, intellectual stimulation, and individualized consideration, is widely believed to effectively promote employees' innovation willingness and output. However, there is still room for exploration in existing research on how transformational leadership is transformed into innovative behavior through the mediation of employees' psychological states.

Using psychological empowerment theory as the core analytical framework, this study systematically examines the influence path of transformational leadership on employee innovative behavior. The study surveyed knowledge workers in technology companies in the Pearl River Delta region, adopting a multi-time-point questionnaire survey design to collect independent and dependent variable data at two time points. A total of 298 valid paired samples were obtained. Using SPSS 26.0 and Mplus 8.3 statistical software, empirical procedures including descriptive statistics, measurement model testing, and structural equation model analysis were completed.

Empirical analysis results reveal that: First, transformational leadership has a significant positive effect on employee innovative behavior (β=0.43, p<0.001), indicating that high transformational leadership can effectively stimulate subordinates' innovative performance. Second, transformational leadership shows significant positive effects on all four dimensions of psychological empowerment (meaning, competence, self-determination, and impact). Third, psychological empowerment plays a significant mediating role between transformational leadership and employee innovative behavior, with a mediation effect value of 0.21 (95% Bootstrap CI: [0.14, 0.29]), accounting for 48.8% of the total effect. Further dimension-level analysis found that the mediating effects of work meaningfulness and self-efficacy are relatively stronger.

**Keywords**: Transformational Leadership; Employee Innovative Behavior; Psychological Empowerment; Knowledge Workers; Mediation Effect
