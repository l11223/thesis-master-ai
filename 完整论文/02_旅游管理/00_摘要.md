# 摘要

## 中文摘要

在体验经济时代，多感官体验已成为旅游目的地吸引游客、提升竞争力的重要手段。然而，多感官体验如何影响游客的目的地依恋，以及这一影响的内在机制，尚缺乏系统的理论解释和实证检验。本研究基于具身认知理论和情绪记忆理论，构建了"多感官体验—情感记忆—目的地依恋"的理论模型，并引入旅游涉入度作为调节变量，系统考察多感官体验对旅游目的地依恋的影响机制。

本研究以福建土楼世界文化遗产地为研究情境，采用问卷调查法收集数据。通过现场拦截和在线回访相结合的方式，共获取有效问卷408份。运用SmartPLS 4.0软件进行偏最小二乘结构方程模型（PLS-SEM）分析，对研究假设进行检验。

研究结果表明：（1）多感官体验对情感记忆具有显著正向影响（β=0.52, p<0.001），其中嗅觉体验的影响最强（β=0.38），其次是味觉体验（β=0.32）、听觉体验（β=0.28）、视觉体验（β=0.25）和触觉体验（β=0.22）；（2）情感记忆对目的地依恋具有显著正向影响（β=0.48, p<0.001），且对地方依赖（β=0.45）和地方认同（β=0.42）均有显著影响；（3）多感官体验对目的地依恋具有显著正向影响（β=0.35, p<0.001）；（4）情感记忆在多感官体验与目的地依恋之间起部分中介作用，间接效应为0.25（95% CI=[0.18, 0.33]）；（5）旅游涉入度正向调节多感官体验与情感记忆之间的关系（β=0.18, p<0.01）。

本研究的理论贡献主要体现在三个方面：第一，将具身认知理论引入旅游目的地依恋研究，揭示了多感官体验影响目的地依恋的具身认知机制；第二，首次系统检验了五种感官体验对目的地依恋的差异化影响，发现嗅觉体验具有最强的情感唤起效应；第三，验证了情感记忆的中介作用，阐明了"感官刺激→情感记忆→地方依恋"的传导路径。本研究的实践启示在于：目的地管理者应注重多感官体验设计，尤其应重视嗅觉营销策略，通过创造独特的感官体验增强游客的目的地依恋。

**关键词**：多感官体验；目的地依恋；情感记忆；具身认知；旅游涉入度

## Abstract

In the era of experience economy, multisensory experience has become an important means for tourism destinations to attract tourists and enhance competitiveness. However, how multisensory experience affects tourists' destination attachment and its underlying mechanism lack systematic theoretical explanation and empirical examination. Based on embodied cognition theory and emotional memory theory, this study constructs a theoretical model of "multisensory experience—emotional memory—destination attachment" and introduces tourism involvement as a moderating variable to systematically examine the mechanism of multisensory experience's impact on destination attachment.

Taking Fujian Tulou World Cultural Heritage Site as the research context, this study collected data through questionnaire survey. A total of 408 valid questionnaires were obtained through a combination of on-site interception and online follow-up. SmartPLS 4.0 software was used to conduct Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) analysis to test the research hypotheses.

The results show that: (1) Multisensory experience has a significant positive effect on emotional memory (β=0.52, p<0.001), with olfactory experience having the strongest impact (β=0.38), followed by gustatory experience (β=0.32), auditory experience (β=0.28), visual experience (β=0.25), and tactile experience (β=0.22); (2) Emotional memory has a significant positive effect on destination attachment (β=0.48, p<0.001), with significant effects on both place dependence (β=0.45) and place identity (β=0.42); (3) Multisensory experience has a significant positive effect on destination attachment (β=0.35, p<0.001); (4) Emotional memory partially mediates the relationship between multisensory experience and destination attachment, with an indirect effect of 0.25 (95% CI=[0.18, 0.33]); (5) Tourism involvement positively moderates the relationship between multisensory experience and emotional memory (β=0.18, p<0.01).

The theoretical contributions are mainly reflected in three aspects: First, by introducing embodied cognition theory into destination attachment research, this study reveals the embodied cognitive mechanism of multisensory experience affecting destination attachment. Second, this study is the first to systematically examine the differential effects of five sensory experiences on destination attachment, finding that olfactory experience has the strongest emotional arousal effect. Third, this study verifies the mediating role of emotional memory and clarifies the transmission path of "sensory stimulation → emotional memory → place attachment." The practical implications suggest that destination managers should pay attention to multisensory experience design, especially olfactory marketing strategies, to enhance tourists' destination attachment through creating unique sensory experiences.

**Keywords**: Multisensory Experience; Destination Attachment; Emotional Memory; Embodied Cognition; Tourism Involvement
