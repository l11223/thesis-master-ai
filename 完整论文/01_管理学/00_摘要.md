# 摘要

## 中文摘要

在数字经济蓬勃发展的时代背景下，数字化转型已成为企业获取竞争优势的关键战略选择。然而，数字化转型如何影响企业创新绩效，以及这一影响的内在机制，尚未得到充分揭示。本研究基于动态能力理论和资源基础观，构建了"数字化转型—组织韧性—创新绩效"的理论模型，并引入环境动态性作为调节变量，系统考察数字化转型对企业创新绩效的影响机制。

本研究采用问卷调查法，以中国制造业企业中高层管理者为调研对象，通过线上线下相结合的方式共收集有效问卷412份。运用SmartPLS 4.0软件进行偏最小二乘结构方程模型（PLS-SEM）分析，对研究假设进行检验。

研究结果表明：（1）数字化转型对企业创新绩效具有显著正向影响（β=0.38, p<0.001），假设H1获得支持；（2）数字化转型对组织韧性具有显著正向影响（β=0.45, p<0.001），假设H2获得支持；（3）组织韧性对创新绩效具有显著正向影响（β=0.42, p<0.001），假设H3获得支持；（4）组织韧性在数字化转型与创新绩效之间起部分中介作用，间接效应为0.19（95% CI=[0.12, 0.27]），假设H4获得支持；（5）环境动态性正向调节数字化转型与组织韧性之间的关系（β=0.15, p<0.01），在高环境动态性条件下，数字化转型对组织韧性的促进作用更强，假设H5获得支持。

本研究的理论贡献主要体现在三个方面：第一，将组织韧性引入数字化转型与创新绩效的关系研究，揭示了"数字化转型→组织韧性→创新绩效"的传导机制，丰富了数字化转型影响效应的理论解释；第二，基于动态能力理论视角，阐明了数字化转型通过增强组织韧性进而提升创新绩效的内在逻辑；第三，识别了环境动态性的边界条件，明确了数字化转型发挥作用的情境因素。本研究的实践启示在于：企业应将数字化转型与组织韧性建设相结合，尤其是在高度动态的环境中，更应加快数字化转型步伐，以韧性能力支撑持续创新。

**关键词**：数字化转型；组织韧性；创新绩效；环境动态性；PLS-SEM

## Abstract

In the era of booming digital economy, digital transformation has become a key strategic choice for enterprises to gain competitive advantages. However, how digital transformation affects corporate innovation performance and its underlying mechanisms have not been fully revealed. Based on dynamic capability theory and resource-based view, this study constructs a theoretical model of "digital transformation—organizational resilience—innovation performance" and introduces environmental dynamism as a moderating variable to systematically examine the mechanism of digital transformation's impact on corporate innovation performance.

This study adopts a questionnaire survey method, targeting middle and senior managers of Chinese manufacturing enterprises. A total of 412 valid questionnaires were collected through a combination of online and offline methods. SmartPLS 4.0 software was used to conduct Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) analysis to test the research hypotheses.

The results show that: (1) Digital transformation has a significant positive effect on innovation performance (β=0.38, p<0.001), supporting H1; (2) Digital transformation has a significant positive effect on organizational resilience (β=0.45, p<0.001), supporting H2; (3) Organizational resilience has a significant positive effect on innovation performance (β=0.42, p<0.001), supporting H3; (4) Organizational resilience partially mediates the relationship between digital transformation and innovation performance, with an indirect effect of 0.19 (95% CI=[0.12, 0.27]), supporting H4; (5) Environmental dynamism positively moderates the relationship between digital transformation and organizational resilience (β=0.15, p<0.01), and the promoting effect of digital transformation on organizational resilience is stronger under high environmental dynamism, supporting H5.

The theoretical contributions of this study are mainly reflected in three aspects: First, by introducing organizational resilience into the relationship between digital transformation and innovation performance, this study reveals the transmission mechanism of "digital transformation → organizational resilience → innovation performance", enriching the theoretical explanation of the effects of digital transformation. Second, from the perspective of dynamic capability theory, this study clarifies the internal logic of digital transformation enhancing innovation performance through strengthening organizational resilience. Third, this study identifies the boundary condition of environmental dynamism and clarifies the contextual factors under which digital transformation works. The practical implications suggest that enterprises should combine digital transformation with organizational resilience building, especially in highly dynamic environments, where they should accelerate digital transformation to support continuous innovation with resilience capabilities.

**Keywords**: Digital Transformation; Organizational Resilience; Innovation Performance; Environmental Dynamism; PLS-SEM
