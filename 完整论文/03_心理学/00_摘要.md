# 摘要

## 中文摘要

社交媒体的普及深刻改变了大学生的社交方式和生活状态，与此同时，大学生群体的焦虑问题日益凸显。社交媒体使用与焦虑之间是否存在关联？其内在机制是什么？哪些因素可能起到保护作用？本研究基于社会比较理论和自我差异理论，构建了"社交媒体使用—上行社会比较—焦虑"的理论模型，并引入自尊作为调节变量，系统考察社交媒体使用对大学生焦虑的影响机制。

本研究以中国大学生为研究对象，采用问卷调查法收集数据。通过线上问卷平台共收集有效问卷526份。运用AMOS 26.0软件进行结构方程模型（SEM）分析，对研究假设进行检验。

研究结果表明：（1）社交媒体使用强度对上行社会比较具有显著正向影响（β=0.42, p<0.001），假设H1获得支持；（2）上行社会比较对焦虑具有显著正向影响（β=0.45, p<0.001），假设H2获得支持；（3）社交媒体使用强度对焦虑具有显著正向影响（β=0.28, p<0.001），假设H3获得支持；（4）上行社会比较在社交媒体使用强度与焦虑之间起部分中介作用，间接效应为0.19（95% CI=[0.13, 0.26]），假设H4获得支持；（5）自尊负向调节上行社会比较与焦虑之间的关系（β=-0.18, p<0.01），即高自尊个体在进行上行社会比较后的焦虑反应较弱，假设H5获得支持。

本研究的理论贡献主要体现在三个方面：第一，将社会比较理论应用于社交媒体使用与心理健康关系的研究，揭示了上行社会比较在社交媒体使用影响焦虑中的中介作用；第二，验证了自尊的保护性调节作用，为识别社交媒体使用的高风险人群提供了理论依据；第三，在中国大学生群体中检验了社会比较理论的适用性，为该理论的跨文化验证提供了证据。本研究的实践启示在于：大学应加强心理健康教育，引导学生理性使用社交媒体，培养积极的社会比较方式，提升自尊水平以缓冲社交媒体使用的负面影响。

**关键词**：社交媒体；焦虑；上行社会比较；自尊；大学生

## Abstract

The popularity of social media has profoundly changed the social interaction patterns and lifestyles of college students, while anxiety problems among this group have become increasingly prominent. Is there a correlation between social media use and anxiety? What is the underlying mechanism? What factors may play a protective role? Based on social comparison theory and self-discrepancy theory, this study constructs a theoretical model of "social media use—upward social comparison—anxiety" and introduces self-esteem as a moderating variable to systematically examine the mechanism of social media use's impact on college students' anxiety.

This study targeted Chinese college students and collected data through questionnaire surveys. A total of 526 valid questionnaires were collected through an online survey platform. AMOS 26.0 software was used to conduct Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) analysis to test the research hypotheses.

The results show that: (1) Social media use intensity has a significant positive effect on upward social comparison (β=0.42, p<0.001), supporting H1; (2) Upward social comparison has a significant positive effect on anxiety (β=0.45, p<0.001), supporting H2; (3) Social media use intensity has a significant positive effect on anxiety (β=0.28, p<0.001), supporting H3; (4) Upward social comparison partially mediates the relationship between social media use intensity and anxiety, with an indirect effect of 0.19 (95% CI=[0.13, 0.26]), supporting H4; (5) Self-esteem negatively moderates the relationship between upward social comparison and anxiety (β=-0.18, p<0.01), meaning that individuals with high self-esteem show weaker anxiety responses after engaging in upward social comparison, supporting H5.

The theoretical contributions are mainly reflected in three aspects: First, by applying social comparison theory to the study of the relationship between social media use and mental health, this study reveals the mediating role of upward social comparison in the impact of social media use on anxiety. Second, by verifying the protective moderating role of self-esteem, this study provides a theoretical basis for identifying high-risk groups of social media use. Third, by testing the applicability of social comparison theory among Chinese college students, this study provides evidence for cross-cultural validation of the theory. The practical implications suggest that universities should strengthen mental health education, guide students to use social media rationally, cultivate positive social comparison patterns, and enhance self-esteem levels to buffer the negative effects of social media use.

**Keywords**: Social Media; Anxiety; Upward Social Comparison; Self-esteem; College Students
